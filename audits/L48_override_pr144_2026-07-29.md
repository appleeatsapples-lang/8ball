## What this override does not cover

The change is additive and unwired, which bounds the damage but does not make
the judgement calls right.

- **Two rulings landed on this branch, and what they leave open is bigger than
  what they closed.** The first collapsed an eleven-entry mode table to nine
  (DOCTRINE §1.B v0.54); the second renamed the driver once collapsing showed
  it to be the **life path** — the same sum under the same reduction the free
  sheet has shown since §1.D v0.38. Both are executed and both are recorded as
  overruling the brief rather than interpreting it. **What neither ruling
  settled, and what a reviewer should press hardest on: the mode of work is now
  selected by a coordinate the free surface already gives away.** Nothing about
  this tier's mode is new information. Whether the public rung wants a driver
  the free sheet does not already show is a design question no one outside this
  lane has read, and it is cheapest to answer now — the table is keyed by one
  integer.

- **Both rulings were executed under the same solo authority as everything else
  here.** Two instructions, one implementer, no second read of either diff: the
  master-stop entries removed, the reduction predicate deleted, the two
  delegating wrappers deleted after that, the output block reshaped from
  `expression` to `mode`, the fixtures regenerated twice. Tests pin that the
  reading's `mode.lifePath` matches `core/profile.js` across the date range and
  that no live line carries the retired label — evidence the paths agree and
  the rename is complete, not evidence that either call was right.

- **The strength model is coarse and this lane cannot judge how coarse.**
  Strength is 旺相休囚死 against the month-branch element alone. A full BaZi
  read weighs roots, hidden stems and the rest of the chart. Whether the
  simplification is acceptable for a date-only tier is a practitioner's call,
  and no practitioner has looked at it. The favourability rankings inside the
  ten entries are likewise conventional but authored — a reviewer who works
  in this tradition might order `[output, wealth, officer]` differently.

- **The tables are authored copy with no second reader.** Fifteen domain-family
  bodies, nine mode methods and twenty-two posture stances were written by
  this lane. They pass the canonical register scans mechanically; nobody has
  read them for taste, for §4 cultural-lineage respect, or for the flattening
  that a five-way partition of all human work invites. The scans catch banned
  vocabulary, not bad judgement.

- **The fixtures were generated from the implementation.** Sixteen snapshot
  cases cannot falsify an engine that was wrong when they were taken. Four
  independent anchors are in the suite to cover that (the three calibrated day
  pillars, a hand-walked case, the sheng/ke re-derivation, and season/posture
  re-derived from the shipped modules) — but they anchor the *composition*,
  not the *convention*. Nothing outside this repository has confirmed that the
  four-step pipeline is a defensible reading of the tradition it cites.

- **The architecture edge is new and unreviewed.** `core/public.js` is the
  first `core/` module to import from `content/`. It is one-way and adds no
  runtime capability, but no second lane has agreed that it is the right shape.

- **The local PII audit did not run.** It needs `audits/local_personal_data.txt`,
  which is gitignored and absent in a fresh container. The in-suite CI PII
  scan passes; the pre-push local audit is outstanding and is controller-side.

- **Scope:** this override covers **#144 only**.

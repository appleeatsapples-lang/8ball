# L48 override — PR #186, merged on a verdict that says "not yet audit-cleared"

This file is the L48 sighting log by construction. **Sighting #22**, and the
first in this chain where a **genuine cross-model verdict exists, was obtained,
and still did not clear the merge** — because the fix it required landed after
it was written and was never re-audited.

That distinction is the whole reason this file exists. The previous twenty-one
sightings record merges with *no* independent read. This one records a merge
with a real independent read that explicitly withheld clearance.

- **PR:** #186 — closes the P0 and two P1s from the 2026-07-29 cross-model deep

- **Base:** `main` at `e3c2586` (PR #185, monochrome UI).

## What WAS independently audited, and what it said

A genuine cross-model pre-merge audit ran against head `b8966fd` and returned
**MERGE WITH FIXES — not yet audit-cleared.** Its full text is preserved
outside this repo in the operator's audit-packet directory; it is *not* filed
here, because filing it would have greened the two artifact gates while its own
text still said the PR was not cleared.

It closed all seven of its original required findings and independently replayed
two false-greens under mutation:

- weakening only the `l48` job's `SHAPE=` now fails **8 of 31** predicate tests,
  where the equivalent mutation previously left the suite green;
- the exact internally-consistent fixture rotation — rotate `source.term_order`,
  relabel every year, regenerate the content digest — now produces 2 validator
  problems, a comparator FAIL, `solarMismatchCount` 2400,
  `semanticViolationCount` 2400 and `termOrderCanonical` false.

It left **one P2 open** and named its required fix precisely.

## What this override actually covers — the gap being waived

**The P2 fix was implemented, and its delta was never audited.**

`3105e4f` closes that P2: `evaluate_hko_comparison()` pinned
`semanticViolationCount` and `termOrderCanonical` by ordinary equality, which is
not a JSON contract in Python (`False == 0`, `1 == True`), so
`semanticViolationCount: false`, `0.0`, and `termOrderCanonical: 1` all cleared
the forged-fixture detector on a type coincidence. It is now a non-boolean `int`
equal to zero, and an identity match on `True`.

The audit's own instruction was explicit:

> Do not replace this verdict with **SAFE TO MERGE** until that evaluator fix is
> on the PR head and its delta has been re-audited.

**The delta `b8966fd..3105e4f` was not re-audited. The controller elected to
merge without it.** That is the specific, and only, clearance gap this override
waives.

Its blast radius is small and worth stating precisely: two files
(`audits/project_audit.py`, `audits/test_project_audit.py`), both inside the
audit tooling, neither shipped to a device. Nothing under `core/`, `ui/`,
`content/` or `index.html` moved in that delta. A defect there would weaken the
auditor's forged-fixture detector; it could not produce a wrong reading.

The fix was mutation-checked by its own author — reverting the pins to
equality-only makes the mutant accept all three named cases while the fixed
evaluator rejects them — but **that is the implementer certifying the
implementer**, which is exactly the arrangement L48 exists to flag.

## Who reviewed

The independent audit is genuine and was not written by the implementing lane.
Two of its findings had been surfaced by the implementer beforehand and were
filed as known-suspect in
`audits/hko_calendar_branch_premerge_audit_2026-07-30_brief.md`; the auditor
confirmed both and found a third the implementer had classified as merely latent
— the comparator taking term order *and* labels from the same fixture, so it was
comparing the fixture to itself. That finding is the strongest argument in this
chain for why the independent read was worth commissioning.

The residual weakness is the reverse of the usual one. It is not that nobody
looked; it is that the person who looked said "fix this first", the fix was
made, and nobody looked again.

## What this override does not cover

- **The unaudited delta above.** Primary gap.
- **A deliberate asymmetry left in the P2 fix.** The older count pins
  (`comparedYears`, `solarComparisons`, `lunarComparisonCount`, and the two
  mismatch counts) reject booleans but still accept floats, so
  `solarComparisons: 2400.0` passes, while `semanticViolationCount` is now
  int-only. The implementer scoped strictly to what the audit required rather
  than widening the delta under review, and flagged it. Nobody has ruled on it.
- **The eight HKO correction values themselves.** The audit confirmed they
  compare cleanly and that the DOCTRINE record around them is now honest about
  its evidence. It did **not** re-fetch the 200 upstream HKO source files. The
  brief identifies that as the highest-leverage available check — the sources
  are reachable and the fixture carries 200 raw-byte digests — and it was not
  performed. The correction table also remains non-minimal by its own logic:
  2085 xiaohan sits 26.4 s from local midnight uncorrected while 2016 xiaoshu at
  237.5 s is corrected.
- **Two red CI contexts at merge time.** `test` and `l48-gate` were both red
  solely at the missing-artifact step; every other step in `test` passed,
  including vitest and the single-file rule. This file is what turns them green,
  and it turns them green by asserting a waiver, not by fixing anything.
- **Scope:** this override covers **#186 only**.
